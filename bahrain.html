Bahrain

Hidden Manama

Beyond the malls and the skyline — the Bahrain that locals actually love.

Home› Destinations› Hidden Manama

Bahrain is one of the most historically rich countries in the Gulf — and most of it sits quietly, waiting to be discovered. Tap each category to explore.

🏰
Forts
Ancient fortresses & UNESCO heritage
⌄

Bahrain Fort (Qal'at al-Bahrain)

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the oldest forts in the Gulf. Layers of civilisation dating back 4,000 years.

Go at golden hour — the light over the sea is unreal.

Arad Fort

A beautifully restored Portuguese-era fort in Muharraq. Small but atmospheric, especially at dusk with the water behind it.

Combine with a walk along the Muharraq corniche.

Riffa Fort

Perched on a cliff overlooking Hunanaiya Valley, this fort was once home to Bahraini rulers. Stunning views and rich history.

Less crowded than Bahrain Fort — a hidden gem.

🌳
Nature
Desert wonders & natural landmarks
⌄

Tree of Life

A 400-year-old mesquite tree standing alone in the middle of the desert — no water source, no explanation. One of Bahrain's most mystical spots.

Best visited at sunset or under a full moon.

🏺
Crafts
Pottery, weaving & traditional arts
⌄

Pottery Village — A'ali

The oldest continuously working pottery village in the Gulf. Watch local craftsmen shape clay the same way their ancestors did centuries ago.

You can try making your own piece at some workshops.

Traditional Sewing & Talli

Talli is Bahrain's ancient embroidery art — intricate metallic thread woven by hand. Look for workshops in Muharraq's old quarter.

A piece of Talli makes the most meaningful souvenir.

🪂
Adventure
Thrills & outdoor experiences
⌄

Gravity Indoor Skydiving

Bahrain's wind tunnel experience — the closest thing to freefall without jumping out of a plane. Great for all ages and levels.

Book in advance — slots fill up fast on weekends.

Horse Riding

Ride through Bahrain's desert landscape — several stables offer guided rides at sunrise or sunset across open terrain.

Early morning rides are cooler and the desert light is beautiful.

Traditional Food
Authentic Bahraini dishes you must try
⌄

Haji

حاجي

A beloved Bahraini breakfast dish — slow-cooked meat served with light bread. Best found in old Manama's traditional restaurants early in the morning.

Jareesh

جريش

Crushed wheat cooked slowly with spices and sometimes meat — a deeply comforting dish that has been eaten in the Gulf for centuries.

Baba Taher

بابا طاهر

A classic Bahraini sweet — soft, syrup-soaked dough balls dusted with sesame. Found at traditional bakeries and street stalls across Manama.

Emmawash

إماواش

A traditional Bahraini fish dish cooked in a rich spiced tomato sauce, served over rice. A true taste of the island's coastal heritage.
