Bahrain

Hidden Manama

Beyond the malls and the skyline — the Bahrain that locals actually love.

Home› Destinations› Hidden Manama

Bahrain is one of the most historically rich countries in the Gulf — and most of it sits quietly, waiting to be discovered. Tap each category to explore.

🏰
Forts
Ancient fortresses & UNESCO heritage
⌄

Bahrain Fort (Qal'at al-Bahrain)

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the oldest forts in the Gulf. Layers of civilisation dating back 4,000 years.

Go at golden hour — the light over the sea is unreal.

Arad Fort

A beautifully restored Portuguese-era fort in Muharraq. Small but atmospheric, especially at dusk with the water behind it.

Combine with a walk along the Muharraq corniche.

Riffa Fort

Perched on a cliff overlooking Hunanaiya Valley, this fort was once home to Bahraini rulers. Stunning views and rich history.

Less crowded than Bahrain Fort — a hidden gem.

Qalaat Bu Maher

A lesser known but fascinating coastal fort in Muharraq — partially submerged at high tide, giving it an almost mythical appearance. One of Bahrain's most photogenic hidden spots.

Visit at low tide so you can walk right up to it.

Bahrain National Museum

Not a fort, but the perfect companion to your historical tour — the museum houses artefacts from Dilmun civilisation and brings Bahrain's ancient story to life beautifully.

Spend at least 2 hours here — there's more than you'd expect.

Nature
Desert wonders & natural landmarks
⌄

Tree of Life

A 400-year-old mesquite tree standing alone in the middle of the desert — no water source, no explanation. One of Bahrain's most mystical spots.

Best visited at sunset or under a full moon.

Al Areen Wildlife Park

Bahrain's only wildlife sanctuary — home to Arabian oryx, gazelles, ostriches and more roaming freely across a protected desert reserve.

Go in the morning when animals are most active and the heat is manageable.

Al Bilaj Beach

One of Bahrain's most serene and unspoilt beaches — far from the crowds, with calm clear water and a raw natural beauty that's hard to find elsewhere on the island.

Perfect for a quiet sunrise or sunset — bring your own refreshments.

Crafts
Pottery, weaving & traditional arts
⌄

Pottery Village — A'ali

The oldest continuously working pottery village in the Gulf. Watch local craftsmen shape clay the same way their ancestors did centuries ago.

You can try making your own piece at some workshops.

Alnaseej Textile Factory

One of Bahrain's most treasured cultural landmarks — a traditional textile factory in Muharraq where craftsmen weave fabric on ancient wooden looms. A living piece of Bahraini heritage.

Go on a weekday morning to see the weavers actively working — it's a truly special experience.

Adventure & Sports
Thrills, outdoor experiences & activities
⌄

Gravity Indoor Skydiving

Bahrain's wind tunnel experience — the closest thing to freefall without jumping out of a plane. Great for all ages and levels.

Book in advance — slots fill up fast on weekends.

Horse Riding

Ride through Bahrain's desert landscape — several stables offer guided rides at sunrise or sunset across open terrain.

Early morning rides are cooler and the desert light is beautiful.

Cycling

Bahrain has a growing cycling scene with dedicated tracks and open coastal roads. Sheikh Nasser Loop in Zallaq is the most scenic route on the island — flat, coastal and stunning at golden hour.

Early Friday mornings are the best time — roads are quiet and cool.

Traditional Food
Authentic Bahraini dishes you must try
⌄

Haji

حاجي

A beloved Bahraini breakfast dish — slow-cooked meat served with light bread. Best found in old Manama's traditional restaurants early in the morning.

Jareesh

جريش

Crushed wheat cooked slowly with spices and sometimes meat — a deeply comforting dish that has been eaten in the Gulf for centuries.

Baba Taher

بابا طاهر

A classic Bahraini sweet — soft, syrup-soaked dough balls dusted with sesame. Found at traditional bakeries and street stalls across Manama.

Emmawash

إماواش

A traditional Bahraini fish dish cooked in a rich spiced tomato sauce, served over rice. A true taste of the island's coastal heritage.

Darseen Cafe

دارسين

A beloved Bahraini breakfast spot inside the National Museum — known for its cinnamon-infused drinks and traditional morning bites in a calm, cultural setting.

Sharbet Saffron Cafe

شربت زعفران

A traditional Bahraini breakfast cafe famous for its saffron drinks and classic morning and lunch dishes. A true local institution that captures the spirit of an authentic Bahraini morning.

Cafes & Markets
Local favourites & hidden gems
⌄

Farmers Market — Hoorat Aali

A vibrant weekly market in the village of Aali featuring fresh local produce, homemade goods, and artisan crafts. A wonderful slice of everyday Bahraini life.

Go early on market day — the best stalls sell out fast.
